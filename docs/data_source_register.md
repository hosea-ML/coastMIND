# CoastMind Data Source Register

## 1. Purpose

This register records candidate sources for the CoastMind coastal-science text corpus. A source must be reviewed before its content is collected or used for model training.

## 3. Review Notes

- SRC-001 is especially relevant because the textbook connects wave and flow processes with sediment transport and coastal morphodynamics.
- SRC-002 and SRC-003 are potential sources of scientifically grounded coastal-hazard explanations and terminology.
- SRC-004 may help broaden the corpus to ocean processes and introductory educational explanations.
- These sources are candidates, not blanket approvals. Record the exact documents selected and their applicable reuse terms before collecting text.
- Do not assume that permission to access or download a document also grants permission to redistribute its full text or use it for every intended purpose.


## 4. Source Status Definitions

- **Pending:** Not yet assessed.
- **Under Review:** Scientific relevance or reuse conditions are being checked.
- **Approved for Use:** Reviewed and suitable for the specified use, subject to recorded conditions.
- **Rejected:** Unsuitable or not permitted for the intended use.

## 5. Collection Rules

1. Do not assume that publicly accessible text is free to reuse.
2. Record the source URL and licence information before collecting content.
3. Preserve required attribution and source metadata.
4. Do not redistribute source documents unless their terms permit it.
5. Keep synthetic examples distinguishable from source-derived text.
6. Record the date of collection and any relevant version information.

## 6. Initial Corpus Plan

The initial pilot corpus will be small and focused on coastal-science fundamentals. Candidate topics include coastal erosion, sediment transport, waves, tides, estuaries, and shoreline change.

The final source selection and corpus size will be decided after the candidate sources have been reviewed.
